# Agent Library Privacy Policy

Draft for review. Effective date: [date of publication].

Agent Library is operated by Crouton Creations, LLC, a Washington state company ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal information when you use the hosted Agent Library service, including its website, workspaces, APIs, and connections to agents and apps (the "Service").

This policy covers the service we operate. If you run the open-source software yourself or use an installation operated by someone else, that operator is responsible for its data practices.

## Information we collect

**Account and sign-in information.** We collect your email address, account identifiers, display label, preferences, and information needed to verify your identity. If you use a sign-in provider, we receive identifiers and account information it supplies for sign-in. We do not receive your password for that provider.

**Content and workspace information.** We store the documents, files, comments, templates, and other material you or your connected agents and apps submit. We also store information needed to organize and manage that content, including titles, folders, version history, authorship, change notes, workspace memberships, invitations, permissions, and connection records. Content may contain personal information about you or other people.

**Billing information.** Our payment provider handles payment details. We receive information needed to manage subscriptions and payments, such as customer and subscription identifiers, plan, payment status, and transaction records. We do not store full payment card numbers.

**Usage and technical information.** We collect information about access to and use of the Service, such as feature interactions, timestamps, account or workspace identifiers, browser and device information, IP addresses, errors, and security events. Some of this information comes from our infrastructure providers. Product analytics may be associated with your account; they are not necessarily anonymous.

**Communications and invitations.** We receive information you send in support requests or other communications. If someone invites you to a workspace, we receive your email address and the invitation details from that person, even if you have not created an account.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Create accounts, verify sign-ins, and manage memberships and permissions.
- Store, organize, search, display, version, and transfer content as requested.
- Operate connections to agents and apps you authorize.
- Manage plans, subscriptions, payments, and service limits.
- Send sign-in messages, invitations, billing notices, and other service communications.
- Provide support, diagnose problems, understand feature usage, and improve the Service.
- Prevent fraud and abuse, investigate security incidents, enforce our terms, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use your private content for advertising or publish it as a product example without your permission.

## Shared workspaces and connected agents

Your content is available to people and connections with permission to access it. In a shared workspace, owners and administrators manage membership and access. Depending on their roles, members can view or change content, connect agents and apps, and see authorship and activity information. Workspace administrators can remove your access. Leaving a workspace does not automatically remove content you contributed to it.

When you authorize an agent or app, we provide the content and related information its permissions allow it to request. A connection with permission to make changes can also submit or change content. Some connections have access to selected projects; others are limited to files the connected app creates.

Revoking a connection or removing a member stops future access through that authorization. It does not delete copies already downloaded, exported, or retained by that person, agent, or app. Their handling of those copies is governed by their own practices and any agreement you have with them.

Ordinary library links require authorized access. Certain viewing and file-transfer features use temporary links that allow whoever holds the link to access the specified content while the link remains valid. Treat those links as confidential.

## AI and your content

Agent Library stores and exchanges material created by people, agents, and apps. We do not use your content to train general-purpose AI models, and we do not send your library to an AI provider merely because you store it with us.

An AI service you connect may receive content through the access you authorize and may process or retain it under its own terms and privacy policy. Review those policies before granting access, especially for sensitive or confidential material.

## When we disclose information

We disclose information in the following circumstances:

- **To provide the Service.** Service providers process information for hosting and storage, authentication, email delivery, payments, analytics, security, and support. They receive information needed for their services and are subject to appropriate contractual restrictions. Some providers, such as payment and sign-in providers, also process information for their own legal and security purposes under their policies.
- **At your direction.** We disclose information to workspace participants, authorized agents and apps, and other recipients you instruct us to send it to.
- **For legal and safety reasons.** We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, address fraud or abuse, protect people or the Service, or establish or defend legal rights.
- **During a business transfer.** Information may be disclosed in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections. We will give notice of a change in ownership or materially different data practices where required.
- **With your permission.** We may disclose information for another purpose when you authorize it.

We may use or disclose aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you. We maintain de-identified information in that form and do not try to re-identify it except as permitted by law.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, protect sign-in flows, remember settings, and understand use of the Service. Product analytics help us understand interactions and performance. We do not intentionally include document bodies, file contents, or unsaved drafts in product analytics or record content through session replay.

Your browser may also store unsaved drafts and copies of content you keep for offline use. These copies are stored on your device and are not end-to-end encrypted by Agent Library. Use care on shared devices. You can remove offline copies through the Service's device controls or clear your browser's site data. Blocking essential cookies or storage may prevent parts of the Service from working. Where required, we ask for consent before using nonessential analytics technologies and provide a way to withdraw it.

## Retention and deletion

We retain account information and content while needed to provide the Service. Version history and items moved to trash remain stored unless permanently deleted; editing a document or moving it to trash does not erase its earlier versions.

You can request deletion of your account or permanent deletion of content by contacting us. After verifying your request and authority over the affected data, we will complete deletion from our active systems within 60 days, or sooner where applicable law requires, except for information we must retain for legal obligations or reasonably need for security, fraud prevention, or legal claims. If an exception applies, we will explain it as required by law. Retained information is limited to those purposes.

Residual copies may remain in restricted backups until those backups expire through their normal retention cycle. We do not use those copies for ordinary service operations. If we restore a backup, we will reapply applicable deletion requests.

We generally retain service audit records for up to one year for security and accountability, unless a longer period is necessary for an investigation or legal obligation. Other operational and security records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for their purposes. Billing and tax records may need to be kept after an account closes. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

Removing your account does not necessarily delete content held in a workspace controlled by another person or organization, or copies retained by connected services. We will consider your rights and the workspace's obligations when handling those requests. We respond to privacy requests within the time required by applicable law; the 60-day deletion period does not extend a shorter legal deadline. Canceling a subscription does not itself delete content, and deleting a workspace does not itself cancel its subscription.

## Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, access controls, and encryption provided by our storage services. The Service is not end-to-end encrypted: our systems must process readable content to provide features such as search and previews. Authorized personnel and service providers may access information as needed to operate, support, and protect the Service or meet legal obligations. No system can guarantee absolute security.

We are based in the United States. We and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries, whose privacy laws may differ from those where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we use the required safeguards.

## Your choices and privacy rights

You can manage available account settings, download content you can access, manage connections, and contact us for help accessing, correcting, exporting, or deleting your personal information.

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, use an authorized agent, appeal a privacy decision, or complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and authority before fulfilling a request. If we decline a request, we will explain the reason and any available appeal process.

For individuals in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, our legal bases, where applicable, are performing our contract with you, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service and understanding its use, complying with legal obligations, and consent where required.

When a business or organization uses a workspace to process personal information on its behalf, it may be responsible for deciding how that information is used, and we may act as its processor or service provider. Contact that organization about information it controls; we will assist as required by applicable law and our agreement. We remain responsible for processing we determine ourselves, such as account administration and security.

## Age requirement

The Service is for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who create or use accounts. If you believe a child has provided information through an account, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Adults remain responsible for having a lawful basis to include information about other people, including children, in their content.
