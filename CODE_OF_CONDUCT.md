# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Agent Library to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and assuming good intent.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub's **Report content** option, or by opening a private security advisory if you can't use that. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they find harmful.
